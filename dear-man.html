DEAR MAN Script Builder

Structure your boundary to neutralize emotional conflict.

Describe (1/7)

State the objective facts of the situation. No judgments, no emotions. Just the undeniable truth.

Express (2/7)

State how this situation impacts you plainly and briefly. Do not over-explain.

Assert (3/7)

State your boundary or request clearly and directly. Do not hint or expect them to guess.

Reinforce (4/7)

Explain the logical, positive outcome of reaching a mutual agreement or respecting your boundary.

Mindful (5/7)

What is your "broken record" phrase? If they attack you emotionally, you will ignore the bait and repeat this.

Appear Confident (6/7)

How will you physically anchor yourself to avoid shrinking or getting defensive?

Negotiate (7/7)

What is a workable, logical compromise you can offer that still completely respects your boundary?
